    Preparation, Disclaimers & FAQ

    What to Bring & First-Timer FAQ

    Bring movement essentials, review venue notices, and check common questions before your first Sunday.

      Important Venue & Schedule Disclaimers
        Do I need prior Kenpo, martial arts, or dance experience?

        Not at all. Both Amara's 18:00 Self-Defence class and Fernando's 19:20 Dance session are taught step-by-step for complete beginners and experienced movers alike.

        Can I come by myself or join only one of the two blocks?

        Yes! Many participants come solo and quickly feel at home. You are welcome for the full evening (18:00–21:30) or for either Self-Defence (18:00) or Dance (19:20).

        Is GroovePark really 100% free?

        Yes—sessions are completely free community gatherings hosted in Amsterdam West in connection with De Regenboog Groep.
