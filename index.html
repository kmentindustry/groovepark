About GroovePark

What is GroovePark?

GroovePark is a free weekly community movement and skill-building space in Amsterdam for the LGBTQ+ community and allies. After our December break, we return on Sundays (18:00–21:30) at Buurtkamer Kostelijk (Tweede Kostverlorenkade 15).
