About the Hub

What is GroovePark?

GroovePark is a free weekly community movement and skill-building programme in Amsterdam for the LGBTQ+ community and allies. Every Wednesday from 18:00 to 20:00 at Buurtkamer Kostelijk—no experience needed, just show up.
