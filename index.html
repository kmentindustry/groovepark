What is GroovePark?

GroovePark is a free weekly community programme for the LGBTQ+ community and allies in Amsterdam. No experience needed. Just show up.

Launch & Location

Wednesday 26 August 2026 — every Wednesday thereafter
18:00 – 20:00
Buurtkamer Kostelijk, Amsterdam West

Offline Directions & Location Map

(Saved on your phone for offline use if your connection drops)

  • Address: Buurtkamer Kostelijk, Amsterdam West
  • Tram/Bus: Take Tram 7 or 13 to nearest stop in West.
  • Landmarks: Located near the canal path on Kostverlorenkade. Look for the entrance flags/posters!

Programme

18:00–18:30Dance & Choreography — all levels welcome
18:30–19:00Fitness Session — body-positive movement
19:00–19:30Self-Defence — safe and supportive
19:30–20:00Ballroom & Vogue Practice — open floor

Preparation & Hygiene

Please bring your own water bottle, clean extra shoes, and a change of clothes. Preparation is highly encouraged!

⚠️ Note: There are no shower facilities available at the venue.
